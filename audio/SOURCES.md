# Test clips

Five fixed 30 s mono clips at 24 kHz, built by `scripts/make_clips.py` from [LibriSpeech](https://www.openslr.org/12) test-clean (Panayotov et al., 2015), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The underlying recordings are LibriVox public-domain audiobooks. Changes: utterances of one speaker joined with 0.35 s gaps, cut to 30 s, resampled 16 kHz to 24 kHz, peak-normalised to 0.7.
